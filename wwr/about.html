White Water Rafting

Our purpose and mission is to connect people with the raw beauty of nature through unforgettable and safe white water rafting experiences while protecting our beautiful waterways.

History

Our journey began in 2004 when two passionate river guides decided to share their love for the rapids with the world. What started with just two inflatable rafts and a small truck has grown into one of the region's most trusted adventure companies.

Over the last two decades, we have safely guided over fifty thousand guests down some of the most thrilling rivers, building a community of adventurers who return to the water year after year.
