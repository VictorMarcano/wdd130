Venezuela

Venezuela is a country of extraordinary natural wealth and geographic diversity located on the northern coast of South America. Its territory spans from the impressive peaks of the Andes Mountains and the vast central plains to paradise-like Caribbean beaches and the ancient tepui formations in the Amazon rainforest, home to Angel Falls, the world's highest waterfall. With a deeply multicultural population, Venezuela is globally known for possessing the planet's largest oil reserves and for a vibrant identity expressed through its music, traditional cuisine like the arepa, and the warmth of its people.
